Our Brand Identity

2TO ERP is an enterprise resource planning system built for organizations that demand precision, security, and scalability. Our brand identity reflects these values through a distinctive corporate shield symbol and the architectural integration of the number "2" as our recognizable corporate asset.

06 — Logo Usage

Do & Don't

Correct Usage

  • Always use approved logo files from the brand kit
  • Maintain clear space around the logo
  • Use flat primary version for most applications
  • Scale proportionally when resizing
  • Use white version on dark backgrounds
  • Place on solid, uncluttered backgrounds
  • Use premium 3D only for presentations/signage

Incorrect Usage

  • Do not stretch, distort, or rotate the logo
  • Do not change the logo colors
  • Do not add effects (glow, blur, outline)
  • Do not place on busy backgrounds without contrast
  • Do not recreate the logo from scratch
  • Do not use the premium 3D version in documents
  • Do not separate the shield from the wordmark in lockup
